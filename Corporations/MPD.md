# 🚓 Miami Police Department (MPD)

The MPD keeps Miami safe. Officers catch wanted players, write tickets, make arrests and send criminals to jail and prison.

---

## 🏢 Rooms

| Room | What it is |
|---|---|
| **MPD Inside** | The **only** room where a shift can start |
| Services, managers office and the other MPD rooms | On-duty staff only. You can't start a shift here |
| Jail | Prisoners wake up in the jail beds |
| Prison | MPD and bounty hunters can arrest from here |
| Prison yard | Coal rocks. Mining cuts the sentence |
| Court | Trials, with a jury called from online players |

> Once you're on duty you can go **anywhere** in the city. Only going **AFK** ends your shift.
> Trying to start work in the wrong room tells you exactly where to go, for example:
> `You can't start your shift in Services - start it in MPD Inside.`

## 📻 The Police Radio

The radio and the **Wanted List** open when you start your shift. You can close them, and they remember where you left them.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Search / click a player** | Picks your target |
| **Charges** | Pick what they did. Each charge adds jail time |
| **Ticket** | They get **30 seconds** to accept or decline. **Declining sends them to jail** |
| **Arrest** | Sends them to jail with the charges you picked |
| **Backup** | Calls other officers to you |
| **Patrol** | Starts your patrol |

---

## ⚖️ Charges

| Jail time | Charges |
|---|---|
| **3 min** | Assault, Escaping, Evading, Gang Homicide, Hacking, Murder, Non-Compliance, Obstruction, Illegal Area, Trespassing, Call Abuse |
| **6 min** | Assaulting an Officer, Drugs, Execution, Terrorism, Jailbreak, Robbery |
| **9 min** | Murdering an Officer |

Charges add up: pick more than one and the times stack.

## ⚡ Stun Gun

- Stuns the target. **No lethal damage.**
- Uses **2 durability** per stun.
- Body armour can **dodge** a stun:

| Vest tier | Stun dodge |
|---|---|
| Gray / Green | – |
| Blue | 5% |
| Purple | 10% |
| Gold | 15% |

---

## 🤖 RoboCop

Click the **police radio** in your inventory, then pick a wanted player from the list. **RoboCop** goes and arrests them for you.

- **Once every 6 hours.** It tells you how long is left.

---

## 🔒 Jail & Prison

- Arrested players wake up in the **jail beds**.
- In the **prison yard**, each piece of **coal** mined takes **1 minute** off their sentence.
- The **one-way gates** only let you through once you've been **released**.
- **Bounty hunters** can arrest players too.

---

## 🪪 Cop ID

If you **lose your Cop ID**, the Chief sends you home. Come back with a new one before your next shift.

## 💻 MPD Hub

| Tab | What it's for |
