## 🙋 Customers

1. 🏦 Go to the **branch** and ask for a teller.
2. 🪪 **Open an account** (you sign an agreement).
3. 💵 A worker on shift can then **deposit, withdraw** or **transfer** for you.
4. 🔐 Rent a **deposit box**: stand next to your safe to open it.
5. 🏠 Buy an **apartment** or an **expansion** at the counter.

> [!NOTE]
> You can **close** your account at the counter too.

## 🏧 ATMs

Click an ATM (stand within **3 tiles**) to take your own money out, no teller needed.

| | |
|---|---|
| 💸 **Fee** | **$5** at the bank, **+$2** for every room away from it (max **$25**) |
| ⏱️ **Wait** | **5 seconds** between withdrawals |
| ⚡ **Quick buttons** | $20 · $30 · $60 · $100 · $250 · $1,000 |
| 📭 **Empty** | An empty ATM can't pay out. Try another one or the branch. |

> [!WARNING]
> 🦹 **Robbing an ATM:** you need a **bat** equipped. Stand still for **30 seconds**. The police are told **right away**. You get **$150–$300**, and your bat loses **half** its durability. Moving, getting knocked down, cuffed or stunned stops it. A robbed ATM shows **static** and can't be robbed again for **30 minutes**.

## 🧹 Working a Shift

| Job | How |
|---|---|
| 🪑 **The counter** | Sit at a teller desk. Your **terminal** opens. Serve customers from it. |
| 💵 **Deposits / withdrawals** | Through the terminal. The customer approves on their screen. |
| 🪪 **New accounts** | The customer signs the agreement. |
| 🏠 **Apartments / expansions** | Sold at the counter. |
| 📋 **Daily Challenges** | **3 random challenges** a day for extra pay, plus a **Gold** one. Find them in the Hub. |

> [!TIP]
> Challenges count teller work, new accounts, apartments sold, ATM refills and tills taken to the vault.

## 🚚 ATM Refill Runs

![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Who-Financial%20Specialist%20%26%20up-2ea44f)

- 📣 When an ATM drops **under $20**, a run pops up for workers on shift.
- ✋ **Claim** it, walk to the ATM and press **Fill** (it fills back up to **$1,500**, paid from the vault).
- 🪙 Press **Collect Fees** to empty the ATM's fee tray. You keep **10%** (**$1–$3**).
- ⏳ Then get back to the bank before your timer runs out, or your shift stops.

## 💵 The Till

![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Who-Supervisors%20%26%20up-e0a020)

| Click | Does |
|---|---|
| 1️⃣ **Single click** | Check how much is in it |
| 2️⃣ **Double-click** | Empty it (needs **$150+**) |

- 🏃 You then have **30 seconds** to carry it to the **vault** in the manager's office.
- 🎁 **5 min** later, everyone on shift gets a **$40 bonus**.
- 💰 Holds up to **$600**, earns about **$25 every 5 min** while someone is on shift.
- ⏳ After it's emptied or robbed, it stays **empty for 30 min**.

> [!WARNING]
> Robbers can rob the till with a **bat**. The police are told.

## 📦 Corp Stock

- The bank **buys Paper** from players at **$4 each** (holds up to **2,500**).
- Paper is used up as the bank does business.
- **Managers** see the stock and the full **sell log** in the Hub.

## 🏢 The Hub

**Click the City Bank icon** (top right, in a bank room).

| Tab | Who can use it |
|---|---|
| 💬 Company chat | Everyone at the bank |
| 📌 Notices | Everyone reads · management posts |
| 📋 Daily Challenges | Everyone |
| 🏖️ Leave (LOA) | Everyone asks · Managers approve |
| 🏧 ATM Machines | Cash in each ATM and refills |
| 👥 Management | Managers: hire, promote, demote, send home |
| 📦 Corp Stock | Managers: stock and sell log |

> [!TIP]
> 🪑 **Managers and Supervisors** can open the Hub off shift by sitting in the bank's **office chairs**.
