# 🎣 Bass Pro

![Bass Pro](https://img.shields.io/badge/Company-Bass%20Pro-2f7d3f?style=for-the-badge)

Bass Pro is Miami's outdoor store: **weapons, body armour, lockpicks and repairs.** It also buys **ore** from players.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> You must be **on shift** to sell or repair.

## 🗄️ The Weapon Rack

- Click the **rack** to see what you can take.
- You only see the items **your rank** can sell.
- Take the item, then sell it to the customer.

---

## 🔨 Repairs (Weaponsmith & up)

1. Take a **Repair Hammer** from the rack.
2. Stand in front of the customer and type **`:repair <name>`**.
3. You both **stand still** and watch the same bar.
4. If **either of you moves**, the repair stops.

> [!NOTE]
> Every landed hit wears weapons and vests down. At **0** they **break and are gone**, so repair them before that.

## 📦 Corp Stock

Bass Pro **buys ore** from players. Step on the **sell point** in the store and the **Sell Stock** window opens.

| Item | Pays |
|---|---|
| ⛏️ Iron ore | **$6** each |
| ⚫ Coal | **$4** each |
| 🔮 Mysterious Stone | **Coming Soon** |

- Bass Pro stops buying when it is **fully stocked**.
- There is a **daily limit** per player.
- Step off the point and the window closes.
