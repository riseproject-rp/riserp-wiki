## 🛒 Shopping

1. 👣 **Step on a shop point** in the Store to open that shop.
2. 👕 **Click an item** to try it on, then **Add to basket**.
3. 🛒 A **cart** follows you while you shop.
4. 💳 **Go to a till spot** and wait. A worker rings you up.
5. 🛍️ After paying you carry an **H&M bag** and get a **receipt**.

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-LTD-f5c518) | Limited stock. When it's gone, it's gone. |
| ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-VIP-8e44ad) | VIP members only. |
| ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-Out%20of%20service-e1000f) | Sold out. Wait for a worker to restock it. |

> [!TIP]
> Nobody working? A 🤖 **sales bot** comes to ring you up.

> [!WARNING]
> You **can't shop while you're on shift** at any job.

## 🧍 Mannequin Outfit Deals

**Double-click a mannequin** in the Store.

| Button | What happens |
|---|---|
| 🟡 **Buy full outfit** | Every item at the **deal price** (cheaper!) |
| 🔴 **Add to cart** | Only the items you clicked, at normal price |

> [!NOTE]
> Some items can **only** be bought from a mannequin.

## 💇 The Salon

1. 💺 **Sit in a salon chair** and pick a hairstyle you own.
2. ✂️ A **Hair Stylist** clicks you. The cut takes **15 seconds**.
3. 🚫 Don't move, or it's cancelled (no charge).

| Hair | Price |
|---|---|
| Bought at H&M | **10%** of its price |
| Any other hair | **$50** |

> [!TIP]
> No stylist around? A 🤖 **hair bot** does it.

> [!CAUTION]
> 🩶 **Gray hair:** 7 days of **online time** after your last haircut, your hair turns gray. Get a haircut to fix it.

## 🧹 Working a Shift

| Job | How |
|---|---|
| 💳 **Ring up** | Customers wait at the till. Trainees can't sell LTD or VIP items. |
| 📦 **Restock** | An item sells out every 5–60 min. **Click the Sold Out sign** and stand by it for **10 sec**. |
| 🧺 **Clean up** | **Double-click** clothes piles and hair on the floor (**5 sec**). |
| 📋 **Daily Quests** | **3 random quests** a day for extra pay. Find them in the Hub. |

## 💵 The Till

![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Who-Supervisors%20%26%20Managers-1e88e5)

| Click | Does |
|---|---|
| 1️⃣ **Single click** | Check how much is in it |
| 2️⃣ **Double-click** | Empty it into the **vault** (needs **$150+**) |

- 💰 Holds up to **$600**, earns about **$25 every 5 min** while someone is on shift.
- 🎁 **5 min** after it's emptied, everyone on shift gets a **$40 bonus**.
- ⏳ After it's emptied or robbed, it stays **empty for 30 min**.

> [!WARNING]
> Robbers can rob the till with a **bat**. The police are told.

> [!TIP]
> 🪑 **Managers and Supervisors** can open the Hub off shift from the **office chairs** in room 21.

## 🛡️ Hotel Staff

![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Rank-7%2B-f5b800)

- 📦 **H&M Inventory** (Hub → Staff): **LTD** and **Regular** tabs. Change prices, add stock, put items on a shelf or in storage.
- 🧍 **Mannequins**: double-click → **Dress in wardrobe** or **Copy my look**, set the **discount %**.
- 👕 **Wardrobe editor**: free / for sale / hidden, and uniforms.
- 📋 **Quest editor** (Hub → Staff).
- 🟢 **On duty** in the Staff database: open **any** company's Hub.
- 🧪 **`:soldout`**: test the sold-out system.
