## 🎯 Starting a Fight

1. 🟢 **Step on the action point** in the Arena Lounge. The Fight Club menu opens.
2. 👥 **Pick a mode**: **1v1**, **2v2** or **3v3**.
3. 🔁 **Pick the rounds**: **1**, **2** or **3**.
4. ✉️ **Invite** your opponent (and teammates). They have **30 seconds** to accept.
5. 🧾 Your fight goes into the **queue**. When it's your turn you're taken **straight into the ring**, about **5 seconds** later.

| Team | Colour |
|---|---|
| ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-Pink-ff3fa4) | One side |
| ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-Blue-20b8ea) | The other side |

> [!TIP]
> Stepping **off** the action point closes the menu.

## 🥊 The Fight

| | |
|---|---|
| ❤️ **Health** | **100** each |
| 👊 **Punch** | **10** damage, one punch a second |
| 🙅 **Miss** | **25%** of punches miss |
| ⏱️ **Round** | **60 seconds** each, up to 3 rounds |
| 💤 **Knockout** | Reach **0** and you're out |

- 🏆 **Win** by knocking out the other side, or by having more health when time runs out.
- 🤝 A **draw** gives everyone's bets back.
- 🛋️ When it's over, everyone goes back to the **sofas**.

---

## 💰 Betting

- Bet **$1, $5, $10 or $25** on the **pink** or **blue** team.
- One bet per fight. You **can't bet on your own fight**.
- Betting closes when the fight starts.

> [!TIP]
> ⭐ **Lucky Devil VIPs** can bet on fights that are still **in the queue**.

---

## 🏅 Prizes

The whole betting pot is split like this:

| Who | Share |
|---|---|
| 🎉 **Winning bettors** | **95%**, split by how much each one bet |
| 🥊 **Winning fighters** | **3%**, split between the team |
| 💵 **Club till** | **1%** |

> [!NOTE]
> If nobody bet on the winner, **everyone gets their money back**.
