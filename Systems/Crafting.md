## ⚙️ How It Works

1. Open the **Workbench** at Bass Pro.
2. Pick a tab: **Weapon Tiers**, **Vest Tiers** or **Crafting**.
3. For tiers, **wear** the weapon or vest you want to upgrade. The bench takes it while it works.
4. Have the **materials** in your bag. The bench shows what you have, e.g. `4/10`.
5. Press **Craft**. Only the materials are taken now. The **price** is paid to a worker when you **pick it up**.
6. It shows in **In progress**, then moves to **Ready for pickup**.

> [!TIP]
> **Hover a tier icon** on the bench to see what that tier gives your weapon and vest.

> [!NOTE]
> **My slots** = how many crafts you can run at once: **2** for everyone, **4** for VIP and staff.

## 🛍️ Picking Up

- **Free** crafts (Molotov) are taken straight off the bench.
- **Paid** crafts wait at the **counter**. A Bass Pro worker sells it to you and you pay then.
- **Nobody on shift?** Have it **sent to you** within 15 minutes, or wait for a worker.

## 💪 What Tiers Do

| Tier | Glow | Vest blocks | Vest hits back | Stun dodge | Weapon extra damage | Weapon blocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ⬜ Gray | No | 4% | – | – | – | – |
| 🟩 Green | No | 7% | – | – | – | 3% |
| 🟦 Blue | No | 11% | 8% chance, 20% of the hit | 5% | +5% | 6% |
| 🟪 Purple | ✨ Yes | 15% | 12% chance, 25% of the hit | 10% | +12% | 10% |
| 🟨 Gold | ✨ Yes | 20% | 18% chance, 30% of the hit | 15% | +25% | 15% |

- Vest block + weapon block add up, but **total blocking is capped at 35%**.
- **Stun dodge** works on stun guns and axe stuns.
- Better tiers also **wear out slower**: Green ~1.3x · Blue ~1.8x · Purple ~2.5x · Gold ~3.6x.

> [!WARNING]
> At **0** durability a weapon or vest **breaks and is gone**. Get it repaired at Bass Pro before then.
