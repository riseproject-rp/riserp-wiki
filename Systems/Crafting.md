# 🛠️ Crafting — The Workbench

![Workbench](https://img.shields.io/badge/Bass%20Pro-Workbench-2f7d3f?style=for-the-badge)

The **Bass Pro Workbench** upgrades your **weapons** and **equipment** to a better tier, and crafts **items**.

**Jump to:** [How It Works](#-how-it-works) · [Tiers](#-tiers) · [What Tiers Do](#-what-tiers-do) · [Durability](#-durability) · [Materials](#-materials)

---

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. Open the **Workbench** at Bass Pro.
2. Pick a tab: **Weapons**, **Equipment** or **Items**.
3. **Equip** the weapon or armour you want to upgrade. The bench upgrades what is in your **equipped slot**.
4. Have the **materials** in your bag. The bench shows how many you have, e.g. `0/6`.
5. Press **Craft**. It goes into **In progress** with its time left.
6. When it's done it moves to **Ready for pickup**. Collect it there.

> [!NOTE]
> **My slots** shows how many crafts you can run at once. VIP players get **4**.

## 🌈 Tiers

Every weapon and armour starts **Gray** (as bought) and goes up one tier at a time:

![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-Gray-9e9e9e) → ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-Green-2ea44f) → ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-Blue-1e88e5) → ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-Purple-8e44ad) → ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-Gold-e0a020)

Each step needs **more and rarer materials**.

---

## 💪 What Tiers Do

| Tier | Glow | Armour blocks | Armour hits back | Weapon extra damage | Weapon blocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gray | No | 4% | – | – | – |
| Green | No | 7% | – | – | 3% |
| Blue | No | 11% | 8% chance, 20% of the hit | +5% | 6% |
| Purple | ✨ Yes | 15% | 12% chance, 25% of the hit | +12% | 10% |
| Gold | ✨ Yes | 20% | 18% chance, 30% of the hit | +25% | 15% |

- Armour block + weapon block add up, but **total blocking is capped at 35%**.
- Example: Gold armour (20%) + Gold weapon (15%) = **35%**.

## 🔧 Durability

Better tiers **wear out slower**:

| Tier | Lasts |
|---|---|
| Gray | 1x |
| Green | ~1.3x |
| Blue | ~1.8x |
| Purple | ~2.5x |
| Gold | ~3.6x |

A worn-out weapon can be fixed by a Bass Pro **Weaponsmith** (`:repair`).
