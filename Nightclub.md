## 🍸 Ordering

1. 🖥️ **Walk up to the drink kiosk** and the menu opens.
2. 👆 **Pick a drink** (one per order) and **chips** (up to 5).
3. 🛒 Press **Checkout**, then **Place Order**.
4. 🫧 A bubble with your order shows over your head.
5. 🙋 A worker brings it to you, and **you pay when it's handed over**.

> [!TIP]
> Nobody takes your order within **5 seconds**? A 🤖 **bartender bot** brings it instead.

> [!NOTE]
> You can **cancel** your order until a worker takes it.

## 🧹 Working a Shift

| Job | How |
|---|---|
| 🙋 **Take orders** | Click a customer's order bubble, or **Accept** on the pop-up. |
| 🍸 **Serve at the bar** | Sell a drink or chips by hand. The customer gets a pink card to accept and pay. |
| 🎟️ **Sell VIP tickets** | Workers only. |
| 🎵 **Sell music chips** | For the jukebox. |
| 🎉 **Confetti** | Double-click to clean. **$15** |
| 🥤 **Drink spills** | Double-click to clean. **$15** |
| 🗑️ **Litter / paper** | Double-click to clean. **$3** |
| ✉️ **Mail** | Pick it up and walk it to the drop-off point. |
| 📋 **Daily Challenges** | **3 random challenges** a day for extra pay, plus a **Gold** one. Find them in the Hub. |

> [!NOTE]
> Mess shows up about **every 7 minutes** while someone is working.

> [!CAUTION]
> 🚫 **No weapons or `:enable` effects while you're on shift.** Off shift, weapons work in the club like anywhere else.

## 💵 The Till

![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Who-Floor%20Managers%20%26%20up-1e88e5)

| Click | Does |
|---|---|
| 1️⃣ **Single click** | Check how much is in it |
| 2️⃣ **Double-click** | Empty it into the **vault** (needs **$150+**) |

- 💰 Holds up to **$600**, earns about **$25 every 5 min** while someone is on shift.
- 🎁 **5 min** after it's emptied, everyone on shift gets a **$40 bonus**.
- ⏳ After it's emptied or robbed, it stays **empty for 30 min**.

> [!WARNING]
> Robbers can rob the till with a **bat**. The police are told.
